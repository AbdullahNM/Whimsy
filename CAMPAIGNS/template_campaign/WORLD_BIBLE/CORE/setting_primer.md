# Setting Primer

The campaign takes place in the **Metro Core**, a dense city-state ringed by automated transit arcs and layered access controls.

## Pillars

- **Contracts drive the plot.** Every opportunity carries a cost, a deadline, or a rival.
- **Resources are finite.** Energy, stress, funds, and reputation all move with your choices.
- **Knowledge is earned.** Public facts are known or suspected; hidden truth must be discovered in play.
- **Consequences persist.** State changes are recorded in `GAMEPLAY/` and never silently reset.
