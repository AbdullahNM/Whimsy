# PUBLIC RELATIONSHIPS

## Key Contacts

- **Inspector Bren** — Senior Harbor Excise Inspector (`Harbor Excise Office`). Strict mentor holding Pier Seven; saw Corin pull the Cinder Ledger from the barge and expects verified proof before midday.
- **Tomas Vesper** — Junior Watch-Clerk (`Harbor Excise Office`). Estranged nephew of Maren Vesper; worked the night-to-dawn tally desk at Pier Seven.
- **Elena Reed** — Dockside Apothecary (`The Lantern Quay`). Childhood friend who confirmed pine-pitch and lamp-oil accelerant in the wheelhouse soot and recalled buying cheap tallow from a shaking deckhand.

- **House Merrow** — Chartered Shipping Barons (Outer Wharf). **Captain Kestrel** and two deckhands have intercepted Corin at the Counting House Gate, demanding to see what came out of *The Redwing*'s wheelhouse.
- **House Vesper** — Bonded Warehouse Factors (Limestone Steps). **Maren Vesper** and two factor-guards are blocking the Pier Seven lane after spotting the charred Cinder Ledger in Corin's satchel.
