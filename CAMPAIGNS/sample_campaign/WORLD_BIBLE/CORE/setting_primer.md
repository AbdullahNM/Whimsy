# Port Vane Setting Primer

**Port Vane** stands at the salt-stained mouth of the Ashwater estuary, built across tiered limestone sea-walls and black slate pilings driven deep into tidal mud. Twice a day the estuary swells twelve feet, turning low stone alleys into canalways and lifting heavy coal-barges level with the timber quays.

## Harbor Law & The Tide-Gate

- **The Iron Harbor Chain**: A heavy forged chain spans the Outer Boom between two squat stone chainhouses. It is winched taut at dusk and lowered only at the midday Tide-Gate slack unless an Excise Master presents a counter-signed warrant.
- **Excise Stamps**: Under the Royal Salt Charter of 412, every crate of refined salt, bale of canvas, and seam of coal entering port must bear a fresh Harbor Excise wax stamp—amber beeswax for general freight, black resin for bonded coal.
- **Pier Seven Customs Shed**: The outer inspection shed where drifting, damaged, or disputed hulls are chained until their manifests match their holds.

## Currency & Trade

Port Vane reckons coin by weight and city mint:

- **Gold Crowns (G)**: Mint-stamped merchant gold; `1G = 20 Silver Marks (S)`.
- **Silver Marks (S)**: Dock wages, warehouse bonds, and customs fines; `1S = 12 Copper Pence (C)`.
- **Copper Pence (C)**: Ferryman fares, tallow candles, ink, and hot broth at the quay stalls.

## Craft & Realism

Port Vane runs on timber, iron, rope, tallow, and salt. There is no arcane spellcraft on the docks; apothecaries mix herbal poultices, pitch, quicklime, and lamp oils using practical craft. Power belongs to whoever controls the wharves, the chain winches, and the wax seals.
