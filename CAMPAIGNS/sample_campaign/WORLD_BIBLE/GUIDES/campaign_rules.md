# Campaign Rules — The Cinder Ledger

## Operational Mode: Classic CYOA

- Select a numbered option (`1`–`4`) at the end of each turn to resolve Corin Hale's next decision.
- Every action carries real consequences across Stamina, Heat, Purse, clocks, and faction standing.

## Clocks & Stakes

1. **Midday Tide-Gate Audit (0/4 — Known)**: Tracks how close Port Vane is to the midday slack tide, when the iron harbor chain lowers and the Chief Tide-Master demands final manifest clearance.
2. **House Interference (0/6 — Suspected)**: Tracks how aggressively House Merrow and House Vesper move to bury, bribe, or seize *The Redwing* evidence.

## Progression & Currency

- **Zero Micro-Scene Rule**: Routine dialogue, short dock walks, and minor inspections give `Delta: 0` progression. Milestone XP is awarded when completing formal investigation phases, uncovering verified primary evidence, or resolving major confrontations.
- **Currency**: `1 Gold Crown (G) = 20 Silver Marks (S)`, `1 Silver Mark (S) = 12 Copper Pence (C)`.
