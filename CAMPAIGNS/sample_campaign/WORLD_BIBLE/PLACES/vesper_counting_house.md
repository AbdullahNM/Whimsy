# Vesper Counting House

Perched atop the dry **Limestone Steps** above the floodline, the **Vesper Counting House** commands a clear view over the entire harbor basin and the Outer Boom chain. Heavy oak shutters, brass-bound doors, and hired watchmen keep dockside mud out of its limestone foyer.

## Architecture & Operations

- **The Lower Salt Vaults**: Thick-walled stone cellars built into the cliffside behind the counting house, kept dry with quicklime pans and iron ventilation grates. House Vesper stores thousands of cured salt-crates here under royal customs bond.
- **The Factor's Gallery**: An upper floor paved in pale stone tiles, furnished with long walnut ledger desks, brass balance-scales, and locked strongboxes holding harbor promissory notes.
- **Maren Vesper's Study**: Overlooks Pier Seven through leaded glass panes; a brass spyglass rests in a wooden cradle on the windowsill.

## Sensory Anchors

- **Sounds**: Crisp rustle of parchment, measured ticking of a weighted pendulum clock, and heavy boots on dry limestone.
- **Smells**: Dry paper, cedarwood oil, sealing wax, and faint mineral dust from the salt cellars below.
