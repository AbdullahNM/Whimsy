# Pier Seven Customs Shed

Built out over the outer estuary on tarred oak beams and wet black slate pilings, **Pier Seven** is the first checkpoint inside the Outer Boom. When fog rolls off the Ashwater, the shed's iron roof drips steadily onto stacked salt-barrels and coiled mooring hawsers.

## Layout & Atmosphere

- **The Tally Porch**: An open timber gallery facing the Outer Boom chain. Brass storm-lanterns hang from iron hooks, casting yellow pools over wet planks and chalked cargo slates.
- **The Clerk's Cage**: A cramped pine-board office inside the shed smelling of damp wool, iron-gall ink, and coal smoke from a rusted brazier. Here sit the bound harbor ledgers, spare wax dies, and the brass seal-presses.
- **The Impound Slip**: A narrow stone berth tucked beside the eastern piling wall, fitted with heavy mooring rings where *The Redwing* now rides low in the grey swell.

## Sensory Anchors

- **Sounds**: Clank of the outer boom links, gulls fighting over crab shells, water slapping the pilings, and the scratch of quill pens against damp ledger paper.
- **Smells**: Wet coal ash, sour estuary mud, beeswax, tarred hemp, and brine.
- **Lighting**: Pale dawn mist cut by whale-oil lanterns and the red coal glow from the clerk's brazier.
