# Inspector Bren

- **Title & Role**: Senior Harbor Excise Inspector, Pier Seven Customs Shed
- **Faction**: Harbor Excise Office
- **Cohort**: `pier_seven_excise`

## Appearance & Mannerisms

A weathered man in his late fifties with grey-streaked whiskers, tar-stained cuffs, and a crooked left thumb broken decades ago hauling seized cargo chains. He wears a heavy oilskin greatcoat pinned with the tarnished brass scale-badge of the Harbor Excise Office and speaks in clipped, unhurried sentences.

## Voice & Demeanor

Bren rarely raises his voice. When annoyed, he taps the brass ferrule of his tally-cane once against the pier planking and waits for a clerk to find the mistake himself. He trusts ink, weights, and unbroken wax far more than merchant house promises.

## Relationship to Corin Hale

Bren took Corin Hale onto Pier Seven on strict probation because Lower Quay boys know tide-tables and hull marks by heart. He expects clean paperwork before the midday Tide-Gate opens and will not shield Corin if a merchant house catches him cutting corners.
