# Maren Vesper

- **Title & Role**: Factor-General of House Vesper
- **Faction**: House Vesper (`house_vesper`)
- **Disambiguation Note**: Never confuse **Maren Vesper** (Factor-General at the Limestone Steps) with her estranged nephew **Tomas Vesper** (Junior Watch-Clerk at Pier Seven).

## Appearance & Mannerisms

Tall, silver-haired, and dressed in dark slate wool lined with dry otter fur, Maren Vesper carries herself with the quiet stiffness of a magistrate. A heavy silver signet ring—not brass—sits on her right forefinger, and she keeps her gloves spotless even on damp harbor mornings.

## Voice & Demeanor

She speaks calmly, never threatening aloud when a polite reminder of bond-tariffs, harbor credit, or the Port Reeve's favor will do the work. She treats customs clerks as minor obstacles to be timed, outwaited, or priced.

## Known Ties

She cut off her nephew **Tomas Vesper**'s branch of the family years ago after a counting-house tally scandal, though she still watches Pier Seven closely whenever House Vesper salt-crates move through the Outer Boom.
