# Elena Reed

- **Title & Role**: Dockside Apothecary & Licensed Compounder
- **Faction**: Independent Guildhall (The Lantern Quay)

## Appearance & Mannerisms

Elena is in her mid-twenties with dark hair pinned back under a waxed linen kerchief, ink-and-herb-stained knuckles, and spectacles tucked into her apron pocket for close weighing. Her wool sleeves always carry the sharp scent of clove oil, pine pitch, and dried sea-fennel.

## Voice & Demeanor

Practical, brisk, and skeptical of tall harbor tales. When Corin Hale brings her an odd scrap of resin, wax, or burned wood, she prefers scraping it onto a slate dish with vinegar and lamp-heat rather than guessing.

## Relationship to Corin Hale

Elena and Corin grew up two alleys apart in the Lower Quay. She patched his ribs when he fell off a collier boom at fourteen, and she still keeps a kettle hot behind her counter when he comes off early watch.
