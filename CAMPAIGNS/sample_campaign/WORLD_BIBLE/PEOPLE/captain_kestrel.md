# Captain Kestrel

- **Title & Role**: Master of the Fast-Packet *Sea Lark*
- **Faction**: House Merrow (`house_merrow`)
- **Cohort**: `merrow_packet_crew`

## Appearance & Mannerisms

Compact and broad-shouldered in a salt-bleached leather jerkin, Captain Kestrel wears brass ear-hoops and keeps a coiled marlinspike strapped to her belt. Her hands are calloused black from hemp hawsers and coal dust, and she walks with the wide, rolling balance of a river pilot.

## Voice & Demeanor

Blunt, impatient, and territorial about the Outer Wharf. She treats customs forms as shore-lubber nuisance and speaks to Excise clerks as if they were dockhands standing in the way of a mooring line.

## Public Standing

Kestrel commands House Merrow's fastest estuary packet boat and often handles urgent cargo disputes for Lord-Captain Hal Merrow before the Port Reeve's clerks even wake up.
