# Harbor Excise & The Merchant Houses

Trade in Port Vane rests on an uneasy three-way balance between the city's customs officers and the two chartered houses that own its ships and cellars.

## Harbor Excise Office

- **Mandate**: Enforces the Royal Salt Charter of 412, collects tonnage and cargo duties, and oversees the Outer Boom chain and midday Tide-Gate.
- **Authority**: Answers to the Port Reeve and Royal Exchequer, though chronic underfunding leaves outer piers like Pier Seven reliant on small crews of inspectors and probationary clerks.
- **Insignia**: A brass pair of scales stamped over a harbor chain link.

## House Merrow

- **Domain**: Coastal colliers, fast estuary packets, tugboats, and the maintenance lease on the iron harbor chain winches at the Outer Wharf.
- **Reputation**: Tough, ship-first, and fiercely protective of their hulls. Merrow captains expect quick dockside clearances and despise Excise delays.
- **Sigil**: A black iron chain link coiled around a gull's wing on grey slate.

## House Vesper

- **Domain**: Bonded limestone salt vaults, customs brokerage, marine credit notes, and dry-storage warehouses along the Limestone Steps.
- **Reputation**: Methodical, well-connected at the Port Reeve's court, and ruthless when protecting bonded monopolies or suppressing irregular paperwork.
- **Sigil**: Brass balance-scales over a split salt-crystal on deep crimson.
