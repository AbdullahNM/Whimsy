Her eyes drop to the scorched leather corner of the **Cinder Ledger** poking past your satchel flap. Before you can answer, heavy sea-boots ring against the quay stones behind you. **Captain Kestrel** strides out of the Outer Wharf mist with a coiled marlinspike at her hip and two Merrow deckhands trailing her shoulder.

"Hold the chain, Factor-General," **Captain Kestrel** barks, planting herself ten paces off with salt-stained fists on her belt. "That Excise boy has been rummaging through a Merrow charter hull all morning, and House Merrow wants a look at whatever burned in that wheelhouse," she finishes. Caught between Maren Vesper's calm guards and Kestrel's impatient crew as the morning fog thins, you grip your brass seal-punch at your belt.
