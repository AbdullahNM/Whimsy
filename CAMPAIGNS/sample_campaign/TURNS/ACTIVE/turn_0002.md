## Player Action
Board The Redwing immediately to inspect the charred wheelhouse and recover the Cinder Ledger.

## GM Response
You swing over the slick impound chain and drop onto *The Redwing*'s wet waist deck, boots crunching on cold coal grit. Fog clings to the tarred hemp shrouds, and the salt-crates along the hatch rest bone-dry under their lashed canvas. Ahead, the wheelhouse door hangs crooked on one blistered iron hinge.

Inside, the burned chart desk smells sharply of pine pitch and sour char rather than coal smoke. You wedge your iron scraper under the swollen top drawer, heave until the wet oak gives, and pull free a leather-bound manifest with blackened edges. Half the middle leaves are ash, yet the back binding still holds a torn crimson-inked tally stub marked `#P7-088`.

Up on Pier Seven, Inspector Bren taps his tally-cane once against the planks as the seven-o'clock harbor bell rings across the estuary. "Log that smudge-book and watch the tide," Bren calls down, gesturing toward the Merrow and Vesper runners still watching from the seawall. You slide the ledger into a waterproof sleeve inside your satchel and climb back up the piling ladder with your shoulders aching.
