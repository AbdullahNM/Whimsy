# Turn 1 — The Cinder Ledger

Salt fog hangs low over Pier Seven, dripping from the iron shed roof onto coils of tarred hemp. Below the wet slate pilings, the burned coal-barge *The Redwing* groans against the impound slip chains. Her wheelhouse is a blackened shell, yet her waist deck still carries three rows of dry, tarpaulin-covered salt-crates stamped with a crisp crimson wax seal.

You button your damp oilskin coat tighter against the estuary chill and check the brass excise seal-punch at your belt. Beside the clerk's brazier, Inspector Bren taps his tally-cane once on the floorboards and jerks his chin toward the blackened hull. "Empty lifeboat davits, no skipper, and half a manifest baked to ash in the desk," Bren says. "Put your probation to work, Corin. I want names and cargo truth before the midday Tide-Gate drops."

Out on the stone seawall, two messengers already watch the slip from under dripping hoods. A Merrow packet runner leans by the winch house, while a Vesper clerk in dry wool waits near the Limestone Steps gate. Inside the clerk's cage, young Tomas Vesper keeps his head bowed over the night tally book, his quill scratching too fast for an empty harbor.
