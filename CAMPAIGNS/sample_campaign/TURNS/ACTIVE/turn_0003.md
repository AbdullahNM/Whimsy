# Turn 3

## Player Action
Slip down the pier steps to The Lantern Quay to consult Elena Reed on the pitch smell and question ferrymen about the drifting barge.

## GM Response
You take the slick stone steps toward **The Lantern Quay**, where whale-oil lamps sputter under timber awnings above the morning fish stalls. Near the Ferry Steps, a squat barge-hand named Pell sits on an overturned salt-keg coiling wet hemp. When you ask about *The Redwing*, Pell spits into the tide and mutters that two strangers in dry oilskins paid silver to ferry three quiet sailors ashore upriver before dawn.

Two doors up, **Elena Reed's Apothecary** smells of clove oil, dried sea-fennel, and vinegar. Elena pushes her spectacles onto her nose and scrapes a speck of black soot from your ledger sleeve onto a slate dish. When she warms it over her bench lamp, the grease catches pale blue and spits sharp resin smoke.

"That is pine pitch mixed with lamp-oil, Corin, not galley coal," Elena says, wiping her brass knife clean on a rag. "Someone lit that wheelhouse on purpose, and I bought cheap tallow from a shaking deckhand outside two nights back," she adds. Through the steamed shop glass, a grey-coated clerk from the **Vesper Counting House** pauses by the quay lantern, spots your excise satchel, and turns briskly up the Limestone Steps.
